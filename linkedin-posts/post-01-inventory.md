🏠 **Building a Home Lab from scratch — Part 1: Inventory**

After years of working with enterprise infrastructure, I decided it was time to build a proper home lab to sharpen my skills and work toward a hybrid cloud setup.

Here's what I'm starting with:

🖥️ **Compute**
- 2x Dell PowerEdge R620 (iDRAC Enterprise)
- 1x HP ProLiant management host
- 1x Dell PowerEdge T330 (storage/NAS)

🔀 **Networking**
- Cisco C891F-K9 — edge router (advipservices licence + VPN hardware module)
- Cisco WS-C3560CG-8PC-S — Top of Rack switch (PoE)
- D-Link DGS-1100-08V2 — out-of-band management
- TP-Link Archer AX12 — Wi-Fi 6

🗄️ **Rack**
- HP 10642G2 — 42U

**The plan:**
Phase 1 → Physical build & structured cabling  
Phase 2 → VMware ESXi + vSphere cluster  
Phase 3 → Infrastructure services (AD, monitoring, Vault)  
Phase 4 → Hybrid cloud (Azure Arc, Terraform, Ansible)

First thing I learned — reset passwords on gear you haven't touched in years 😅
